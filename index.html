How the Parametric Form Works

A compact 12-constant analytical representation captures H105 to within 0.000086 c RMS while exposing decoupled, unimodal physical knobs for thickness, camber, peak locations, nose radius, and trailing-edge wedge/cusp angles.

Chapter 1

Anatomy of H105 & Orthogonal Construction

Low-Reynolds-number hydrofoils like H105 (Re ≈ 105–5×105) balance laminar rooftop extent against mild pressure recovery to avoid laminar separation bubbles. H105 places its 12.58% peak thickness at xt/c ≈ 0.379, carries aft-loaded 1.68% camber peaking at xc/c ≈ 0.646, enters with a fine leading-edge radius rLE/c ≈ 0.00459, and exits with θTE ≈ −8.81° so the lower surface rises above the chord line into a slight aft cusp (yl > 0 near x/c ≈ 0.97).

Instead of adding vertical thickness ±yt(x) (which skews the nose radius when θLE ≠ 0), the half-thickness yt(ξ) is offset perpendicular to the mean camber curve (ξ, yc(ξ)) along unit normal (−sin θc, cos θc), where tan θc = dyc/dξ. This guarantees exact G2 leading-edge curvature continuity with osculating radius rLE = ½ B02 cos θLE centered at (rLE cos θLE, rLE sin θLE).

xu,l(ξ) = c [ ξ ∓ yt(ξ) sin θc(ξ) ], &qquad yu,l(ξ) = c [ yc(ξ) ± yt(ξ) cos θc(ξ) ], &qquad cos θc = 1√(1 + (yc')2)
rLE = 12 B02 cos θLE, &qquad B0 = limξ→0+ yt(ξ)√ξ

Chapter 2

The 12-Weight Bernstein / Kulfan Master Profiles

Standard pure CST thickness bases struggle with sections like H105 that combine a fine nose radius (rLE/t2 ≈ 0.29) with a fuller forward shoulder, causing alternating positive/negative Bernstein weights. Adding a single integer-power nose slope mode bnose η(1 − η)6 cleanly separates the √η nose radius from the shoulder fullness, allowing just 7 thickness weights and 5 camber weights to match all 65 points of H105Coord.csv to 0.000086 c RMS.

Yt,raw(η) = √η (1 − η) ∑k=05 bk C(5,k) ηk(1 − η)5−k + bnose η(1 − η)6
Yc,raw(η) = η (1 − η) ∑k=04 ck C(4,k) ηk(1 − η)4−k

Chapter 3

Peak Relocation via Möbius Domain Warps

Moving a section’s maximum thickness or camber location by re-solving polynomial weights often introduces secondary humps or negative thickness pockets. Instead, we compose the baseline master curve with a bilinear rational diffeomorphism (Möbius warp) on [0, 1] → [0, 1] that maps the target peak coordinate xtarget identically onto the baseline peak η0.

γ = η0(1 − xtarget)xtarget(1 − η0), &qquad η(ξ) = γ ξ1 + (γ − 1) ξ, &qquad dηdξ = γ[1 + (γ − 1) ξ]2 > 0

Because dη/dξ > 0 everywhere on [0, 1], every strictly unimodal master curve remains strictly unimodal with exact peak value t (or f) locked at xt (or xc).

Chapter 4

Decoupled Endpoints via One-Sided C2 Hermite Modifiers

To control leading-edge radius rLE, trailing-edge wedge angle βTE, and camber angles (θLE, θTE) independently without shifting peak location, peak magnitude, or creating spurious humps across η0, we add one-sided cubic Hermite modifiers supported strictly on [0, η0] (nose) or [η0, 1] (tail).

ΦLE(η) = √η (1 − η/ηt,0)3  (for η ≤ ηt,0, else 0), &qquad ΦTE(η) = (1 − η) σt3  (for η ≥ ηt,0, else 0), &quad σt = η − ηt,01 − ηt,0
ΨLE(η) = η (1 − η/ηc,0)3  (for η ≤ ηc,0, else 0), &qquad ΨTE(η) = (1 − η) σc3  (for η ≥ ηc,0, else 0), &quad σc = η − ηc,01 − ηc,0

Vanishing to cubic order at η0 keeps value, first derivative, and second derivative identically zero across the peak, guaranteeing global C2 smoothness while morphing H105’s aft cusp (θTE = −8.81°) cleanly through flat (θTE = 0°) to reflexed (+4°).

Figure 4. Left: One-sided modifiers ΦLE(η) and ΦTE(η) join flat to order C2 at ηt,0. Right: Sweeping θTE from −14° to +4° morphs the aft cusp continuously into reflex while leaving forward geometry (ξ ≤ xc) unchanged.
Chapter 5

Validation: Coordinate Fit & Low-Re Hydrodynamic Polars

Across all 65 coordinates of H105Coord.csv, the default parameterization achieves an RMS perpendicular error of 0.000086 c and a maximum error of 0.000223 c. Low-Reynolds-number viscous polars computed with NeuralFoil (xlarge, α ∈ [−2°, 8°]) confirm close aerodynamic equivalence across Re = 1×105, 2×105, and 5×105 (max |ΔCL| ≤ 0.0072, max |ΔCD/CD| ≤ 1.28%) and illustrate how the design knobs shape the laminar drag bucket and pitching moment.
